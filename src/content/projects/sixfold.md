### About the Project

**SixFold** is a personal browser game built around 100 rolls of weighted dice. It is free to play and non-commercial; its in-game Play Gold has no monetary value.

### Details & Status

* **Status**: Published and available to play
* **Platform**: Web browsers
* **Play**: [Open SixFold](https://dongho9601.github.io/sixfold/)
