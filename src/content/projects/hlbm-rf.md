### About the Project

**Hold Less, Be More: Rune Foundry (HLBM-RF)** is a browser-first spatial incremental and pattern-construction game set within the broader HLBM design space.

The game focuses on spatial placement of power sources, engine routing, recipe binding, and milestone unlocks.

### Details & Status

* **Status**: In active development
* **Platform**: Modern Web Browsers
* **Stack**: TypeScript, Vite, HTML5 Canvas, Vitest
* **Core Loop**: Heart power routing $\rightarrow$ Engine activation $\rightarrow$ Recipe binding $\rightarrow$ Shards & upgrades $\rightarrow$ Frontier expansion
