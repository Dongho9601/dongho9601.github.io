### About the Project

**Hold Less, Be More (HLBM)** is a mobile tactical tetromino puzzle RPG and dungeon crawler featuring roguelite progression. 

Players navigate dungeon encounters through spatial block-placement mechanics, tactical ability activations, and run-based equipment builds.

### Details & Status

* **Status**: In active development
* **Platform**: Mobile (portrait-first) & WebGL test builds
* **Engine / Stack**: Unity, C#
* **Core Mechanics**: Tetromino board puzzle mechanics merged with tactical turn-based dungeon crawling
